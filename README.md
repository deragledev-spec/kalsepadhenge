# KalSePadhunga 📚

**Your JKBOSE Previous Year Question Paper Finder.**

KalSePadhunga is an independent, student-focused platform built to make finding JKBOSE Previous Year Question Papers easier, faster, and more accessible.

Students often spend unnecessary time searching across multiple websites just to find a particular question paper. KalSePadhunga aims to simplify that experience by bringing available papers together in one organized place.

Find question papers by class, subject, and year — without unnecessary searching.

---

## 🎯 The Problem We're Solving

Preparing for board examinations is already challenging. Finding the right study resources shouldn't make it harder.

JKBOSE students often face problems such as:

- Previous year question papers are scattered across different websites and sources.
- Finding a specific subject's paper from a particular year can be time-consuming.
- Some websites have confusing navigation, making it difficult to locate the actual papers.
- Students may spend more time searching for resources than using them for preparation.

These may seem like small problems, but they affect the everyday study experience of students.

## 💡 Our Solution

KalSePadhunga is designed to make discovering previous year question papers straightforward.

Through a simple interface, students can:

- Browse available JKBOSE question papers.
- Select their class and subject.
- Filter papers by examination year.
- Access the original source pages containing the papers.

**Our philosophy is simple: Students should spend less time finding resources and more time learning.**

## ✨ Features

- **Class-based filtering:** Explore available papers for different classes.
- **Subject filtering:** Find question papers for specific subjects.
- **Year filtering:** Discover papers from available examination years.
- **Original source links:** Access question papers through their original source pages.
- **Simple user interface:** A straightforward experience without unnecessary navigation.
- **Responsive design:** Designed to work across different screen sizes.
